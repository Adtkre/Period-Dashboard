      Model Architecture & Biological Realism

      Discussion of baseline comparison, uncertainty, and grouping

      1. Random Forest vs Dummy Baseline: The Random Forest classifier scores 38.9% accuracy and a macro F1 of 0.386, outperforming the most-frequent class dummy baseline (30.3% accuracy, 0.155 macro F1).

      2. Biological Noise & Follicular Plasticity: Unlike artificial benchmarks, physiological menstrual cycle duration exhibits natural cycle-to-cycle dispersion of ±6.8 days even within identical individuals. Ovulation timing is susceptible to acute stress, sleep disruption, and hypothalamic signaling.

      3. Discrete Category vs Date Mapping: The classification pipeline categorizes cycles into three clinical windows (<33d, 33-41d, >=42d). Translating this discrete classification into an exact calendar date carries an expected uncertainty range of ±2 to ±3 days, faithfully reflected in our calendar confidence interval.
